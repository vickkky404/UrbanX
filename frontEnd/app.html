Frequently Asked Questions

How do I book a ride?

Enter your pickup and drop-off locations, select your preferred vehicle type, and tap "Request Ride". A nearby captain will be assigned to you.

How do I cancel a ride?

Go to "My Rides", find your active ride, and tap the cancel button. Note that cancellation fees may apply if the captain has already started.

How do I add money to wallet?

Go to the Wallet section and tap "Add Money". You can add funds using UPI, cards, or net banking.

How do I report an issue with a ride?

Go to "My Rides", select the ride, and tap "Report Issue". You can also contact our support team directly.
