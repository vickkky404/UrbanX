Our Story

UrbanX started with a simple idea: transportation should be seamless, affordable, and sustainable for everyone. What began as a small initiative to connect commuters has grown into a city-wide movement redefining urban mobility.

We believe in the power of technology to bridge gaps. By integrating bikes, autos, and carpooling into a single platform, we aren't just booking rides; we are building a community that moves together.
