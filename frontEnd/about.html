The UrbanX Story

It all started in 2023 with a shared frustration: why is it so hard to find a reliable ride during rush hour? What began as a university project has now grown into a comprehensive mobility solution.

We didn't just build an app; we built a bridge between commuters and captains. By focusing on transparency and fair pricing, we've created a ecosystem where everyone wins.
